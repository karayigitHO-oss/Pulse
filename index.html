Words don't diminish
when they're given away.

Pulse began as four friends in Ankara writing to a shared theme, out loud, from seven in the evening until whenever the theme runs out. It's grown to include friends in Syracuse, a bookbinder giving these words a body, and anyone who wants to leave something in the air for someone else to find.

Gatherings

Each session lands on a theme, sometimes on purpose, sometimes by accident. What comes out of it is kept here roughly as it arrived, translated where it needs to be, untouched where translation would flatten it.

Session — Ankara / online theme: köprü / bridge

The Bridge

Our meetings sparked from the idea of 'poetics of relationality,' and this relationality is represented by the 'bridge' we hope to make with people originating from different cities, and writing about everyday life. The idea of 'bridge' as an abstract but also a material concept came out of a conversation about whether these gatherings would even matter,. We believe there's something quietly fitting about an act of connecting. So, rather than saying 'this happened by accident,' we hope to build bridges that formally enact future accidental connections through abstractions/concepts/themes/terms...

Kaan Gürgan · NOM theme: what a word owes you

Sözcükler

On words as things you can leave somewhere and not be lessened by leaving.

Türkçe

Buraya birkaç sözcük bırakıyorum Çünkü, benim değil sözcükler Havalandırıyorum onları, ya da Bir alıcısı çıkar belki Peki, alan ne yapar? Oysa, eksilmez sözcüklerim … Bu sözcükler döküldü buralara Birazdan toplarım, buradan da Bir şey eksilmez

English

I leave a few words here because the words are not mine I air them out, or maybe a receiver will turn up Well, what does the receiver do? Yet my words are not diminished … These words spilled here, to this place I'll gather them soon, from here too Nothing is diminished

Written in Turkish, 09.08  ·  translated into English for Pulse
