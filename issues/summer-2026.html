translation

I leave a few words here because the words are not mine I air them out, or maybe a taker will take them Well, what does the taker do? Yet my words are not diminished So they multiply, they spread Do they wander in circles and find their way back to me? Am I the home of these words? I am a point Words pass through me and move on So that I won't grow heavy where they gather I leave them Because I don't want to grow heavy How can something heavy spread, scatter? I want lightness, so that I can fly away To the places my words have scattered to, let me go, in whatever direction Let me find my words, let me meet them Maybe let me meet words that someone else has left, and say these, or ones like them, had I too left them? Let me be so light that I'm won over by whatever I meet Let me linger with them, let me blend into them Let me see myself in others, let mirrors lose their function Let reflections gather in someone's eyes, in their footsteps, in their words, and where they gather, let them spill onto the ground Let me gather them, let me think they're mine If, at the same moment, we both reach for the same reflection, no one, in the end, is lessened What spills from anyone, in the end, is not lessened So even if we share it, it isn't diminished These words spilled here, to this place I'll gather them soon, from here too Nothing is diminished
