# Photographs

Put image files for the Materials section in this folder.

Claude cannot save an image that was pasted into a chat — it can look at
one, but not write it to disk. So photographs have to be dropped in here
by hand, then wired into the page.

## Naming

Lowercase, no spaces, describing what it is:

    notebook-01.jpg
    notebook-coptic-spine.jpg
    kevin-thompson-paper.jpg

## Size

Save at roughly **1200px** on the long edge and export as JPEG at around
80% quality. Photographs straight off a phone are often 4–6 MB, which is
far more than a web page needs and makes the site slow to open on a
phone connection. A well-sized version is usually 200–400 KB.

## Once a file is in here

Tell Claude the filename and which card it belongs to. The placeholder
swatch gets swapped for the real picture, with alt text written for
anyone using a screen reader.

## Permission

Only put photographs in here that the collective has the right to
publish. NOM Atölye's product photographs are Yalım's work, so they need
his explicit okay, not just his awareness — once they are in the repo
they are public and can be copied by anyone.

Screenshots of Google Maps or Street View should not go on the site.
Google's imagery is licensed and republishing it on a public page is not
covered by that licence. If a photograph of the Katip Çelebi building is
wanted, someone in the group should take one.
